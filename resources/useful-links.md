## Tips for Learning

1. **Start with Free Tier**: Practice without spending money
2. **Hands-on Practice**: Build projects, don't just read
3. **Follow along Tutorials**: Implement what you learn
4. **Join Communities**: Ask questions and share knowledge
5. **Build Projects**: Apply knowledge to real scenarios
6. **Stay Updated**: AWS releases new features constantly
7. **Get Certified**: Validate your knowledge
8. **Clean Up Resources**: Avoid unexpected charges
9. **Use Tags**: Organize and track resources
10. **Document Learning**: Keep notes and share knowledge
